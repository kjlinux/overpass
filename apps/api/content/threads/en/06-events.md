Announce meetups, hackathons, conferences and tech communities across the continent, online or on site.

For each announcement: the city or "online", the date, the registration link, and whether it is free. Write-ups after the event are as useful as the announcements.

Overpass was born for Systalink's 2026 African Developers Cup. If you are taking part in a competition, say so: feedback between teams belongs here.
