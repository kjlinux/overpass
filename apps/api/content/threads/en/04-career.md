Finding clients, setting prices, getting paid on time, growing: everything about the trade belongs here.

A few rules to keep this topic useful:

- Figures are welcome when they come with context: country, type of client, year. A rate without context helps nobody.
- No job offers and no prospecting: Overpass is not a marketplace.
- Negative experiences with a client or employer stay factual and anonymised.

Your public profile shows your itineraries, your checks and your points. It is concrete proof of work you can share with a client.
