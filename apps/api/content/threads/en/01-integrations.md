This topic is for plugging a service in and making it work: mobile payments, SMS, USSD, or any other African API.

To get a fast answer:

- Name the service, the country and your stack in the title. "Orange Money CI, Laravel 11: the notification never arrives" beats "Webhook problem".
- Paste the error message in its own field and the relevant code in the other. The form masks keys and tokens pasted by mistake, but read it again anyway.
- If an itinerary exists for your case, open the thread from the itinerary with "It does not work for me": it will be linked to the service and the country.

When an answer solves your problem, accept it. And if you learnt something the documentation does not say, turn the thread into an itinerary: that is how the catalogue grows.
