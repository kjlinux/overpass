language: en

Show what you are building, ask for honest feedback, look for a hand.

To present a project: one sentence on the problem it solves, the link, the stack, and what you want feedback on. Screenshots beat promises.

To ask for help: describe what you have already tried. If the blocker is an API, the Integrations and APIs topic fits better and lets you link an itinerary.

A project that ends up as an itinerary or an article on your Overpass blog earns points and helps the next person.
