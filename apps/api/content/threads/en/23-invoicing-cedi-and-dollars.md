Hello,

I landed my first remote client, a small company in Belgium. We agreed on a monthly retainer but we have not discussed currency. They suggested euros, my bank account is in cedis, and transfer fees look scary.

How do you handle invoicing for clients abroad? Which currency, which payment method, and how do you avoid waiting weeks for the money?

@@ author=kofi-builds at=2026-08-22T18:20 useful=ama-mensah,chidi-eth,serge-tech accepted @@
Invoice in the client's currency, so the price stays clean for them, and state the payment terms on the invoice itself, for example 14 days. Ask whether they can cover the transfer fees so that you receive the full amount, many do when it is written clearly.

For receiving, compare what you actually get in cedis for the same amount through two or three services. The difference is often bigger than the fee shown.

@@ author=chidi-eth at=2026-08-22T20:05 useful=ama-mensah @@
Send the invoice on the last working day of the month, not when you remember. A fixed rhythm gets you paid on a fixed rhythm.

@@ author=kwame-dev at=2026-08-26T09:30 useful=ama-mensah @@
Keep a separate account or wallet for the foreign currency money and convert only when you need to. The rate moves a lot, and you can choose a good day.
