Overpass was built for the African Developers Cup, the Systalink Challenge 2026, on the theme "Build the African exchange platform, for devs, by devs". Source: the official CADev page on systalink.com and the challenge cockpit, consulted on 23 October 2026.

## The deliverables

- A public git repository.
- An online demo, usable without signing up so that it can be understood in thirty seconds.
- All of it submitted before 25 October 2026 at 17:00 GMT.

## The constraints

- An active Systalink product is mandatory: Overpass runs on a Datacloud cloud server and a .africa domain.
- Open source technologies only. The team went further and allowed permissive licences only (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD, ISC, PostgreSQL), with an automatic audit on every commit and the report kept in the repository.
- A team of two to five members. Ghostapi has two.

## The ranking

The public, the participants and the jury vote. Hence a product useful from the first user: a simulated payment end to end without an account, itineraries that state their condition, and real usage figures on the home page, never invented.

## What we learnt along the way

Three rules served us more than any technology: one source of truth (the specification), no fabricated data that looks like usage, and a log of every use of AI in the build. The first two protect the members' trust; the third protects ours.

If you are taking part in a competition, the "Events and communities" discussion topic is there to exchange between teams.
