Overpass is the exchange of verified African integrations. Developers share what the official documentation does not say: the code that works, the known pitfalls, and checks dated by other developers.

## Three building blocks

**Itineraries.** Integration sheets filed by service, country and stack. Each one shows its state: clear when members checked it recently, to verify when nobody has confirmed it, reported broken when an open pothole blocks it. The state is never typed in: it is computed from the checks.

**The Simulator.** A simulator that replays the real cases of a mobile money payment without a merchant account: Wave, PayDunya, CinetPay and PI-SPI to start with. Signed webhooks reach your machine with a single command, no tunnel, no certificate.

**The MCP server.** It serves the itineraries to coding assistants, so the AI in your editor writes the right local integration instead of an imaginary one.

## What you find today

- Fifteen services in the catalogue, with the countries where their public documentation confirms a presence, each with its source and consultation date.
- Ten itineraries written by the team from official documentation and our own production integrations. They are all "to verify": it is up to you to confirm them or report a pothole.
- Six discussion topics, a blog per member, and a weekly newsletter.

## What depends on you

An unconfirmed country for an operator becomes confirmed as soon as an itinerary for that service in that country turns clear. The map of covered countries is yours to complete. If a service is missing, propose it; if a simulator is missing, vote for it.

Overpass is the project of team Ghostapi, Côte d'Ivoire, for Systalink's 2026 African Developers Cup. The code is public under a permissive licence, and every use of AI in building it is logged in the repository.
